Note+ is a handwriting notes app for students and professionals, with notebooks that stay in sync on all your devices as you write.

Write on one device and watch the ink appear on the others. Every stroke, page, image and line of text is sent as it happens, and changes made offline are merged when you reconnect. Two people typing in the same text box end up with both of their changes.

A library that looks like your bookshelf
• Notebooks with colored covers or a preview of their first page
• Favorites and search, synced with your account
• Open several notebooks as tabs and switch between them in one tap

Writing that feels natural
• Fountain pen, ballpoint, pencil and highlighter, with pressure
• Partial eraser that only removes what's under it, or a whole-stroke eraser
• Lasso to move, recolor and resize your handwriting
• Shapes, straight lines, images, PDFs and typed text
• Scroll through your pages, or turn them one at a time like a real notebook

Study smarter
• Flashcards with spaced repetition, made from your own pages
• Tape to hide answers and reveal them with a tap

Your notes, your server
Note+ syncs through a small server that you can host yourself, so your notes never have to leave your hands. It is free and open-source software (GPL-3.0), based on Saber by Adil Hanney.
